Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Study Quest is a small, free study guide made by Logan MacManus, a student. It is a personal project. It is not run by, or affiliated with, any school or company.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to send you a sign-in code and to let the owner approve or decline your access.
  • A 3-letter callsign you choose, shown on the public leaderboard to other approved users.
  • Your study results: score, XP, level, best test or quiz percentage, Knowledge studied percentage and quiz accuracy, shown on the leaderboard.
  • Time studied: the minutes a guide was open and in use (the page visible and touched, clicked, scrolled or typed in during the last minute). It is counted on your device, saved to your account and shown on the leaderboard. Idle or background tabs do not count.
  • Access details: whether you were approved, denied or removed, and the dates.
  • Suggestions and problem reports you choose to send from the suggestion button: your message, which guide or page you were on, the question it is about (if any), the time, and your account. They are stored with your account until they are deleted.
  • Answer statistics: when you finish a quiz or test, the site adds to a total of how many times each question was answered right or wrong. These totals are per question, not a record of your own answers, and they are used to find questions that need fixing.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, payment details or anything else. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics, tracking or advertising. It uses your browser's local storage only for things the site needs: keeping you signed in, and remembering your settings (theme colors, sound and animation choices, your progress and callsign). Your study progress is kept on your device and also saved to your account so it follows you between devices; it is readable only by you. The Back up button saves a copy to a file on your device. Because this is strictly necessary, there is no cookie banner. Clearing your browser data or choosing Sign out removes it.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase stores accounts, leaderboard data and the study content, and handles sign-in.
  • An email delivery service sends the sign-in code (through a Gmail account) and the approval request to the owner (through Resend). The approval request contains your email address.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web pages and, like any host, may log visitors' IP addresses.

Fonts are hosted on this site; nothing is loaded from Google or other third parties.

Who can see it

Other approved users see your callsign and results on the leaderboard. Only the owner can see the list of email addresses and approvals, and only the owner can read suggestions and problem reports and the answer totals. Study questions are only available to signed-in, approved users.

How long we keep it

Approval lasts 30 days, after which you must ask again. Your email, callsign, results and any suggestions you sent are kept while this study guide is in use, and are deleted when you ask or when the project ends.

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete your data, or to remove your callsign from the leaderboard, at any time by emailing loganshanebusiness@gmail.com. This site is meant for college students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
